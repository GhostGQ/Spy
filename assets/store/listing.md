## English

```
SPY — which one of you isn't who they claim to be?

Gather your friends, pass the phone around and work out who the spy is. Every player knows the secret word — except the spy. Their job is to blend in, yours is to expose them through questions and answers. Perfect for parties, hangouts and family evenings.

🕵️ How to play
One device for the whole group — nothing else needed. Each player looks at their role and word in turn, then passes the phone on. Once everyone has seen their card the discussion begins: ask each other clever questions to smoke out the spy, without handing them the word. It ends with a vote and the big reveal.

🎮 Two game modes
• Classic — civilians know the word, spies don't. You choose how many spies.
• Chaos — the number of spies is hidden and randomised. There may even be more spies than civilians!

New modes coming soon: Ghost, Syndicate and Detective.

✨ Features
• 3 to 12 players on a single device
• Word categories: locations, food and cities — more in the works
• Built-in discussion timer and hint questions
• English and Russian language support
• No internet, no sign-up, no accounts — play anywhere
• Simple rules you'll pick up in the first round

Gather your friends and find out who can bluff — and who can see right through a spy.
```
